Objectif du Projet

Analyse et visualisation des données de ventes de Mozo Hunt

Ce projet m’a permis de tirer parti de Power BI non seulement pour l’analyse de données, mais également pour concevoir des interfaces interactives orientées utilisateur. J’ai exploré comment intégrer de la logique métier complexe dans un outil BI, tout en veillant à l’ergonomie et à l’impact visuel.

1. Data Collection & Cleaning

The data was collected from the sales system and exported to Excel. Cleaning included handling missing values, standardizing date formats, and validating payment methods.

2. Data Modeling

With Power Query, the data was transformed into a suitable model: separated tables for orders, customers, and payments with proper relationships defined.

3. KPIs & Metrics

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as Total Customers, Total Orders, Revenue, and Units Sold were computed using DAX.

4. Data Visualization

A responsive dashboard was built in Power BI including donut charts (subscriptions), funnel charts (payment methods), and a time series bar chart for monthly revenue.

5. Insights & Business Value

The dashboard revealed Google Pay as the most preferred payment method and April 2024 as the peak revenue month. Such insights can drive marketing and operational decisions.

🛠️ Tools Used

  • ✔️ Microsoft Excel
  • ✔️ Power BI
  • ✔️ Power Query
  • ✔️ DAX for measures and KPIs
